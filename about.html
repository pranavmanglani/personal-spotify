About this project

Music.
My way.

A personal music library built around the simple idea that listening to music should feel effortless, personal and distraction-free.

01 — The idea

Made for one listener.

This isn't another social platform or streaming service. It's my own personal music space — a place where I can keep the songs I actually listen to, find them quickly and play them without unnecessary distractions.

The project is intentionally simple: a clean interface, a personal library and music that is always within reach.

03 — The philosophy

No noise. Just music.

“The best music player is the one that gets out of the way and lets the music play.”

— The idea behind this project

No feeds. No recommendations trying to decide what I should hear next. No unnecessary features. Just a library that belongs to me.
